The story

Governed data and business documents, in one conversation

Adventure Works Cycles makes and sells bikes through resellers and online. Its numbers live in four Qlik apps; its rules, plans and customer voice live in policies, contracts, manuals and reviews. Qlik Answers reads both, and shows where they disagree.

New single-agent skills

Descriptive through to Prescriptive, Simulation and Optimization, with Blind Spots surfacing what the current selection hides.

Customer-defined skills

Teach the assistant how Adventure Works talks

Two plain-language skills make answers follow house conventions. The feature is pre-release, so final screens may differ.

Adventure Works calendar

Defines
The fiscal year runs 1 July to 30 June and is named by the year it ends; Q1 is July to September.
Instruction
"This year", "last year", "last quarter" and "year to date" mean fiscal periods unless someone says "calendar".

Recent sales defaults

Defines
"Recent" means the last 6 full calendar months.
Business rule
If no time period is given, use the last 6 full months.
Instruction
State the period used at the start of the answer; report money in US dollars.

About the data

  • Microsoft AdventureWorks OLTP sample, held in Snowflake and loaded into four Qlik Cloud apps on UKDemoSaaS.
  • Dates are shifted forward by whole quarters so the latest data sits at the most recent quarter end.
  • All 60 documents and 5,000 website comments are synthetic, written for this demo.

Watch-outs

  • The latest quarter has few reseller orders, so several territories show low attainment. Lead with Northeast.
  • Bike demand ends 30 Aug 2026; frame the forecast as "from the last full season".
  • There are no leavers in the HR data, so steer away from attrition.
