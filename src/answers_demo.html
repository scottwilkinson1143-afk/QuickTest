The story

One review, three leaders, one assistant

Adventure Works Cycles makes and sells bikes through resellers and online. Its numbers live in four Qlik apps; its rules, plans and customer voice live in policies, contracts, reviews and website feedback. With the FY2027 Q1 business review a week away, the Sales Director, the Marketing Manager and the HR Business Manager each prepare with the same assistant. Each track ends on a moment where a document and the data disagree, and the finale pulls their findings into one review.

The demo is modular: a 2-minute opening, three 7-minute tracks and a 3-minute finale. Run all of it in about 25 minutes, or pick the tracks that suit the audience.

Customer-defined skills

Teach the assistant how Adventure Works talks

Three plain-language skills make answers follow house conventions: one sets the fiscal calendar, one sets what "recent" means, and one sets how every answer is laid out. The feature is pre-release, so final screens may differ.

aw-adventure-works-calendar

Adventure Works calendar

Used for any question that mentions a year, quarter, year to date or other period.

Defines
The fiscal year runs 1 July to 30 June and is named by the year it ends: FY2027 is July 2026 to June 2027. Q1 is July to September.
Business rule
"This year", "last year", "last quarter" and "year to date" mean fiscal periods unless someone says "calendar". A comparison with last year uses the same span of the previous fiscal year.
Instruction
Name the fiscal period and its calendar months, for example "FY2027 Q1 (July to September 2026)".
aw-recent-sales-defaults

Recent sales defaults

Used when a question says "recent" or gives no time period.

Defines
"Recent" means the latest 6 months of data, counted back from the last month with orders, not from today's date.
Business rule
If no time period is given, use the recent period. Quota questions use the last 2 fiscal quarters with sales, because quotas have no monthly date.
Instruction
State the period at the start with its month names, for example "Over the latest 6 months of data (March to August 2026)". Report money in US dollars.
concise-structured-answers

Concise structured answers

Used for every data or policy answer, including follow-ups and repeated questions.

Format
A bold one-line lead, then three to five bullets with one figure each. App figures and document content sit in separate labelled sections.
Citations
At most one citation per sentence or bullet, attached only to what that source says.
Numbers
Money in dollars with no cents on amounts of $100 or more, ratios as percentages, and no expressions, field names or other technical working.

Also possible: per-app calculation skills. A skill can be scoped to a single app to hold its calculation rules, such as which master measures to prefer, how the tables relate and which business rules apply when a question leaves something open. This demo doesn't need them, because the master measure descriptions already carry those rules, but they're worth raising with customers whose apps have few governed master items.

About the data

  • Microsoft AdventureWorks OLTP sample, held in Snowflake and loaded into four Qlik Cloud apps on UKDemoSaaS.
  • Dates are shifted forward by whole quarters so the latest data sits at the most recent quarter end.
  • All 60 documents and 5,000 website comments are synthetic, written for this demo.

Watch-outs

  • The latest quarter has few reseller orders, so several territories show low attainment. Lead with Northeast.
  • Bike demand ends 30 Aug 2026; frame the forecast as "from the last full season".
  • There are no leavers in the HR data, so steer away from attrition.
