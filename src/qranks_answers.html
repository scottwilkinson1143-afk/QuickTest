The story

One review, three leaders, one assistant

Qranks makes and sells bikes through resellers and online. Its numbers live in four Qlik apps; its rules, plans and customer voice live in policies, contracts, reviews and website feedback. With the FY2027 Q1 business review a week away, the Sales Director, the Marketing Manager and the HR Business Manager each prepare with the same assistant. Each track ends on a moment where a document and the data disagree. The demo is modular: a 3-minute opening and three 9-minute tracks, about 30 minutes in all, or pick the tracks that suit the audience.

Customer-defined skills

Teach the assistant how Qranks talks

Three plain-language skills set the fiscal calendar, what "recent" means and how every answer is laid out. The feature is pre-release, so final screens may differ.

aw-adventure-works-calendar

Qranks calendar

Any question that mentions a year, quarter or year to date.

Defines
Fiscal year runs July to June, named by the year it ends (FY2027 = Jul 2026 to Jun 2027). Q1 is July to September.
Rule
"This year", "last quarter" and "year to date" are fiscal unless someone says "calendar".
Instruction
Name the period and its months: "FY2027 Q1 (July to September 2026)".
aw-recent-sales-defaults

Recent sales defaults

A question says "recent" or gives no time period.

Defines
"Recent" is the latest 6 months of data, counted back from the last month with orders, not from today.
Rule
Quota questions use the last 2 fiscal quarters with sales, as quotas have no month.
Instruction
State the period first: "the latest 6 months of data (March to August 2026)". Money in US dollars.
concise-structured-answers

Concise structured answers

Every data or policy answer, including follow-ups.

Format
Bold one-line lead, then 3 to 5 bullets with one figure each. App figures and documents in separate sections.
Citations
At most one per bullet, only for what that source says.
Numbers
No cents on $100 or more, ratios as percentages, no field names or expressions.

Also possible: per-app calculation skills. A skill scoped to one app can hold its calculation rules. Not needed here, because the master measure descriptions carry them; worth raising where apps have few governed master items.

About the data

  • Microsoft AdventureWorks OLTP sample, held in Snowflake and loaded into four Qlik Cloud apps on UKDemoSaaS.
  • Dates are shifted forward by whole quarters so the latest data sits at the most recent quarter end.
  • All 60 documents and 5,000 website comments are synthetic, written for this demo.

Watch-outs

  • The latest quarter has few reseller orders, so several territories show low attainment. Lead with Northeast.
  • The forecast beat (A3) needs re-engineering: Answers says the data does not support a forecast.
  • There are no leavers in the HR data, so steer away from attrition.
